About Us

Animeverse joined the Little Tokyo community in 2018 with a goal of providing our customers with an experience that’s inclusive, diverse, and welcoming to all who enjoy anime at any level. Our website provides a wide variety of most popular anime shows and we strive to set the highest standards when it comes to our products by assuring that our figures are authentically licensed with perfect detailing and design.

Animeverse is an international business focused on creating both online and offline experiences for fans through content (licensed, co-produced, originals, distribution), merchandise, events, gaming, news, and more. Animeverse connects anime and manga fans across 200+ countries and territories through the content they love. Best known as a top streaming service delivering AVOD and SVOD content, Animeverse also provides experiences to deepen fan engagement and community through social, events, games, consumer products, content distribution, content creation, and manga publishing.
